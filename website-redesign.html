Outdated Visual Design

Old colors, weak section backgrounds, basic cards, poor button styling, and outdated spacing can make a website look less trustworthy. I improve the design so the website feels modern and clean.

02

Bad Mobile Responsiveness

If your website looks good on desktop but breaks on mobile, visitors may leave quickly. I fix mobile spacing, stacked layouts, text sizes, image behavior, buttons, and section alignment.

03

Weak Content Flow

A website should guide visitors from headline to services, trust, proof, and contact. I improve section order and content hierarchy so visitors understand what you offer faster.

Broken grids, overlapping text, stretched images, wrong alignment, plugin styling issues, and spacing bugs make websites look unfinished. I clean and repair those layout problems.

Before / After Improvements

What changes during a professional website redesign?

A redesign is not only about changing colors. A good redesign improves the full website experience: first impression, content clarity, section structure, mobile layout, typography, buttons, trust, and the path visitors follow before contacting you.

Before 01

Weak first impression

The hero section may look outdated, unclear, empty, or overloaded with text. Visitors may not quickly understand what the website offers or what action to take.

After

A stronger hero section with clear headline, short message, better spacing, professional visual balance, and visible call-to-action buttons.

Before 02

Broken mobile layout

Text may be too large, buttons may overflow, images may crop badly, and columns may not stack properly on mobile or tablet screens.

After

A responsive layout tested across mobile, tablet, laptop, and desktop with clean spacing, readable text, proper image behavior, and fixed alignment.

Before 03

Messy section flow

Website sections may feel random, with no clear order from introduction to services, proof, benefits, process, FAQ, and contact.

After

A clean content journey that guides visitors from the main message to services, trust-building content, project proof, and a final contact action.

Before 04

Low visual trust

Poor typography, weak colors, inconsistent buttons, basic cards, and cramped spacing can make a website look unfinished or less professional.

After

Better typography, consistent colors, clean cards, polished buttons, improved spacing, modern section backgrounds, and a more trustworthy visual style.
