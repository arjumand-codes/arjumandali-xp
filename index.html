What a website actually needs to work.

Before hiring anyone, here's what I think separates a website that gets results from one that just sits online.

A Quick Guide
Essentials Five things I check on every build
01

A clear message above the fold

A visitor should understand what you do and who it's for within a few seconds — no guessing, no jargon.

02

Fast load times

Compressed images, minimal scripts, and clean code so the site loads quickly on both desktop and mobile connections.

03

A mobile-first layout

Most visitors arrive on a phone first — the mobile version gets designed and tested with just as much care as desktop.

04

One obvious call to action

Every page should point toward a single next step — book a call, send a message, or make a purchase — not five competing buttons.

05

Content you can actually edit

Clean WordPress and Elementor structure means you can update text, swap images, and add pages without needing a developer for every change.
